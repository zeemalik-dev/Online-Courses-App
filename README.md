## Grow your skills. Make your next move.

WILAN Learn brings practical leadership and professional development courses to your phone. Learn at your own pace, build skills you can put to work, and keep your learning journey organized in one place.

### Learn what moves you forward

Explore courses and programs across topics such as:

- Leadership and career development
- Confidence, communication, and influence
- Workplace dynamics and personal branding
- Time management and financial wellbeing

Course availability and formats may change. Visit [wilaninstitute.app](https://wilaninstitute.app/) to see the latest programs.

### Your learning, in one app

- Discover courses and browse by category
- Watch lessons and work through structured course content
- Keep track of your enrolled courses and learning progress
- Access your account and learning materials on the go

## Start learning

1. [Explore WILAN programs](https://wilaninstitute.app/) to find a course that fits your goals.
2. [Download WILAN Learn on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.wilaninstitute.learn).
3. Sign in or create an account to access your learning.

## About WILAN

The Women in Leadership Advancement Network (WILAN) supports women across Africa with practical learning and leadership development. WILAN Learn is part of that journey: helping you build knowledge, strengthen your skills, and take your next step with confidence.
